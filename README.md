# Pocket Empire

A tiny turn-based strategy game for the browser — built for fun, inspired by
old "lite" mobile 4X games (the kind that used to ship under 2MB on a phone).
Grow a city, research tech, build an army, and beat a rival empire either by
capturing its capital or by simply out-developing it before the turn limit.

**[Play it live](https://eljoe45.github.io/pocket-empire/)**

## How to play

- Tap your city (🏰) to open the build menu — train units, put up buildings,
  or grow the city.
- Tap one of your units, then tap a highlighted tile to move or attack.
- Science adds up every turn and unlocks new tech automatically — check the
  **Tech** panel to see progress.
- Win by capturing the rival capital, or have the stronger empire when the
  50-turn limit is reached.

## What's in it

- A procedurally generated 9×9 map (plains, forest, hills, water), regenerated
  each new game.
- A small tech tree (Archery, Masonry, Currency, Riding) that unlocks new
  units and buildings as your science accumulates.
- Simple turn-based combat with terrain bonuses, ranged units, and counter-
  attacks.
- A basic AI opponent that builds, researches, and marches on your city.

Built with plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no build step, no dependencies,
and light enough to install as a home-screen shortcut on a phone.
