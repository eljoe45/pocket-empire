Pocket Empire

Grow an empire in your pocket

A tiny turn-based strategy game inspired by old lite mobile 4X games: grow your city, research tech, build an army, and beat a rival empire — either by conquest or by simply out-developing them.

  • Tap your city to build units and buildings.
  • Tap a unit, then tap a tile to move or attack.
  • Research unlocks automatically as your science adds up.
  • Win by capturing the enemy city — or have the stronger empire after 50 turns.
